Built for future IT professionals

ITVerse is a digital learning resource designed for Bachelor of Science in Information Technology students. It brings organized computing lessons, practical examples, activities and references together so you can understand important IT concepts at your own pace.

  • Lessons organized by year level
  • Practical examples in every book
  • Activities and quizzes to check your learning
  • A glossary and references for quick lookups
